What happens to your receipts

Last updated November 19, 2026

What Tallyable keeps

Your Google email (only to keep your club's books apart from everyone else's), your club's name and members, your events, and your receipt photos. They're saved on the Tallyable server so they're still there next time you sign in.

Who else sees it

  • Receipt photos go to Anthropic's Claude so it can read them. Anthropic doesn't train its models on what apps send it.
  • Your event name goes to Claude too, for Tally's one-line reaction.
  • If you link a Google Sheet, your rows get written there. That sheet is yours, so its sharing settings are up to you.

What Tallyable doesn't do

No ads, no selling data, no tracking. The only cookies are the ones that keep you signed in and keep the app secure.

Deleting

Delete an event in the app and it's gone from the server too. Want everything wiped, club and all? Email kevchoi@ucdavis.edu and it's done within a week.

The fine print

Tallyable is a free student project, provided as is, with no warranty. Double-check the numbers before you pay anyone back!
