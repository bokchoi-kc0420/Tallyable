# Treasurer interviews: 5 people, 20 minutes each

**Goal:** before week 2, find out whether the event close-out pain (reimbursement plus funder reports) is real for clubs other than KSEA.
**Who:** five treasurers or finance officers from different UC Davis clubs. Mix in at least one club that uses a CSI agency account and one that has received a CFC or ORG grant.
**Rules:** ask about the last real event, not about hypotheticals. Don't pitch the tool until the last question. Don't collect their real names, amounts, or receipts. Take notes as "Club 1 to 5".

## Questions
1. Walk me through the last event you paid for. What happened between buying stuff and the books being done?
2. Where do the receipts end up? Who collects them?
3. Who paid out of pocket? How did they get paid back, and how long did it take?
4. Did any of the money come from a grant (CFC, ORG, national org, department)? What did they ask for afterward?
5. What's the last money mistake you or a past treasurer made? How was it found?
6. How long does closing out an event take you? What's the slowest part?
7. What tools do you use? (Sheets, Venmo, Splitwise, campus forms, ChatGPT?) What do you wish they did?
8. If something prepared the receipts and reimbursements for you, what would you need to trust it?
9. *(Now show the review-screen sketch.)* What would stop you from using this next event?
